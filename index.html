    ADRs for AI — getting started #

    A guided, checkable walkthrough that takes you from a bare machine to your first deliberatively-built project: a small game, live on the internet, built with a model collaborator — and the decisions behind it on the record, where you can find them again.

    Three chapters, one path:

    1. Why are we doing this? — one page, before any terminal.
    2. Setting up your tools — Homebrew to Claude Code, each with its why. (~25–40 min)
    3. Your first project — template → recruit → first decisions → live on GitHub Pages. (~45–60 min)

    Every step says how to verify it worked, your checkmarks persist in your browser (they never leave it), and the All tasks page shows the whole trail in one place.

    You’re among the first to walk this guide. Where it doesn’t match what you see, that’s the guide failing you, not the reverse — every step carries a one-click “this doesn’t match what I see” link, and that feedback is part of the design.
