    The debrief — a record, like everything else ~5 min debrief

    ☰

    Builds on: Your first answers

    You just practiced putting decisions on the record. The last move is to put the walk itself on the record — and this one is ungraded, and will stay that way: it calibrates the guide, never you. You are among its first walkers; your stall points are worth more to us than your successes.

    Ask your collaborator:

    Before we wrap: please write docs/adr/debrief-first-walk.md — a short record of this first walk, from both of us. Four sections: what we built and the decisions on the record; roughly how long each chapter actually took me; where the guide didn't match what we saw (step IDs if we noted them); and what you'd tell the next pair who walks this. Mark it clearly as ungraded feedback for the guide's authors. Then commit it.

    Then get it to us, either way is complete:

    • Share: open a walk report and paste your repository link (private is fine — add jlumbroso as a reader), or
    • Summarize: same form, paste the debrief’s text instead of the link. Keeping your repo to yourself is a fine choice and costs nothing.

    One honesty note: a walk-report issue is public (it lives on the guide’s repository). Prefer private? Email works and is equal: course walkers, reply to the announcement; anyone else, the maintainer’s email is on their GitHub profile — three lines: where you stopped, what it said, what you expected.

    ✓ You know it worked when: a docs/adr/debrief-first-walk.md exists in your project, and we have either its link or its summary
